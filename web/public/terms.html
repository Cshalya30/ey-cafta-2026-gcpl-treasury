Terms of use

GCPL Treasury Command · last reviewed 18 October 2026

Do not rely on anything in this application for a live transfer. Every figure it displays is generated by a seeded script. No balance, rate, facility or counterparty position corresponds to a real one.

1. What this is

A demonstration artefact built for the EY CAFTA 2026 case championship. It renders a proposed treasury operating model as working software so that the controls can be examined rather than asserted.

2. What it is not

  1. It is not a Treasury Management System and must never be described as one. No organisation of this scale should build its own.
  2. It is not a payment-initiating system. It has no payment rail, no bank connectivity and no credentials. It proposes, validates and records.
  3. It is not a system of record for accounting. The ERPs remain the books.
  4. It is not a production system and carries no production data.
  5. It is not financial, tax or legal advice. The regulatory citations it displays are provided so a reader can check the source themselves, and several are flagged in the documentation as requiring verification against the current gazette before being presented.

3. The company is invented

Global Consumer Products Ltd and every subsidiary named here are fictional. No real listed issuer appears anywhere in the dataset, and an automated test fails the build if one is introduced. Bank names are real, because bank panels are public, but no invented financial position is attributed to any bank as its own.

4. Regulatory citations

Citations are reproduced so that the reasoning can be audited, not as a substitute for advice. Rule 10TD of the Income-tax Rules 1962 is cited with its sub-rule (3B) applicability note because renumbering under the Rules 2026 is unconfirmed. The IFSCA GRCTC framework date and the current ECB all-in-cost ceiling are likewise flagged for verification in docs/PRD.md, Appendix D.

5. Acceptable use

Run it, read it, and take it apart. Do not present its output as real financial information about any real organisation, and do not remove the synthetic-data ribbon: it is load-bearing, and a test asserts its presence on every route.

6. No warranty

Provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. The authors accept no liability for any decision taken in reliance on it.
